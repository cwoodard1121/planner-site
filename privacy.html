Planner privacy policy

Planner is a personal study planner built and used by one person (the owner). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Planner reads and writes the owner's Google Calendar so it can show, create and move study and work blocks. It stores its own planning data (settings, task completion, session timing) on the owner's own server.

What it stores

The owner's Google sign-in refresh token is stored encrypted on that server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Calendar text that the owner asks the AI assistant about is sent to the AI provider (OpenRouter) only to produce a reply.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account settings and delete the server's data directory.
